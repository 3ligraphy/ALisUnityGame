**Use this in "What's New in This Version" or "Release notes":**

---

## Reviewer Notes (for App Store Connect / Google Play)

**Use this in "Notes for Review" or "Internal testing notes":**

---

**App name:** Failaka Island  
**Developer:** Failaka Games  
**Version:** 1.2.0  
**No login or account required.** The app can be tested immediately after launch.

**How to test:**

1. **Launch** — Open the app; you’ll see the intro, then the main map/menu.
2. **Navigation** — Use the first-person controls (virtual joystick / touch) to move. Tap scene buttons or gates to enter Museum, Wells, Gemstones, or Cinema.
3. **Exhibits** — **Double-tap** on exhibit objects (e.g. in the Museum) to open the info popup. Tap close or outside to dismiss.
4. **Info icons** — Blue info icons appear above points of interest. Tap an icon to open the related info popup.
5. **Cinema** — In the Cinema scene, **double-tap** the video screen to enter fullscreen. Use the **minimize** button (top-right) to exit fullscreen.
6. **Encryption** — The app does not use custom encryption (only standard HTTPS). Export compliance: ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = false.
7. **Content** — Educational/cultural content about Failaka Island (Kuwait). No user-generated content, no social features, no in-app purchases.

**Optional:** If you have a low-end device, the app uses a Quality Manager that adjusts graphics for performance; no user action required.

**Contact:** For any review questions, use the contact details provided in the developer account.
